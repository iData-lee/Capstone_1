# Capstone 1 - EmporiUm Student Bookstore (Maryland) Analysis

## Project Overview
EmporiUm is a student bookstore chain that sells books, technology, art
supplies, and other products in-store and online. In this project I acted as a
data analyst for the **Maryland** sales territory. I used SQL in MySQL Workbench
to answer the sales manager's questions, visualized results with Excel
PivotCharts, and made a recommendation for next quarter.

## Sales Territory Analyzed
- **Territory:** Maryland (MD)
- **Region:** Northeast
- **Territory type:** In-store (9 stores)
- **Regional Director:** Michael Jarvis
- **Sales Manager:** Shruti Reddy
- **Data period:** January 1, 2022 to December 31, 2025

## Key Findings
1. **Total revenue:** $11,451,615.09
2. **Revenue trend:** $2.41M (2022), $2.81M (2023), $2.82M (2024), $3.41M (2025). February dipped in 2023, 2024, and 2025.
3. **Regional comparison:** Maryland is 47.25% of the Northeast's $24,237,526.98.
4. **Categories:** 89,466 transactions. Technology & Accessories leads with 16,836 transactions and about 71% of revenue at the eight stores other than North Harford.
5. **Stores:** North Harford produces about 76% of Maryland revenue ($8,708,119). Annapolis and Queen Anne's County are the two lowest of the other eight, below their average of about $343K.

## Recommendation
Focus on **Annapolis and Queen Anne's County** in January to March 2026,
prioritizing Technology & Accessories, and have the plan in place before
February, which has dipped in each of the last three years. The main benefit is
reducing Maryland's dependence on one store.
